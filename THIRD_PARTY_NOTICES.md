## 1. Bundled binaries

### Google Protocol Buffers (protobuf) 3.21.12
- **What:** Runtime serialization library used to deserialize neural-network
  metadata. Shipped as a compiled assembly.
- **Where:** `Runtime/ThirdParty/Protobuf-3.21.12/Google.Protobuf.dll`
  (generated C# in `Runtime/Inference/MetaGraph.cs`).
- **Note:** This assembly is **not** the stock NuGet binary. It is the
  `netstandard2.0` build of Google.Protobuf 3.21.12 with
  `System.Runtime.CompilerServices.Unsafe` 6.0.0 merged into it as internal
  types (see the next entry), because Unity's Mono scripting profile does not
  ship that assembly on any Unity 6 version. The merge is performed with
  ILRepack `/internalize`; Google's code is otherwise unmodified. Its strong
  name is dropped as a consequence of repacking.

- **Source:** https://github.com/protocolbuffers/protobuf
